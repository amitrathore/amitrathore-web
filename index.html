I’m an engineer, entrepreneur, and author exploring intelligence, consciousness, and how we create value together.

Those interests run through the software, the companies, and the writing. This is a place to follow the connections between them.

01 / The practice

Making things.

Software gives an idea somewhere to go. A question can become a model, a protocol, a working system—and eventually something people use. Engineering is the practice that lets me make that journey.

AwakeVC is a single-person effort. I’ve built nearly all the software across the platforms, apps, and ventures incubated here, with a great deal of help from coding agents.

That help matters. Coding agents help me write, test, debug, and improve the software. I set the direction, make the architecture and engineering decisions, review the work, and remain accountable for what ships. I’m grateful for the reach they give a single builder.

One person. Many agents.
More room to make things.

The current work explores systems in which people and agents share context, contribute, and carry work forward together. It brings an old engineering question into a new setting: how do the pieces cooperate?

02 / The connections

The thread through
the companies.

My work has taken me through chip-design simulation, enterprise systems, machine learning, commerce, and publishing. The scale and the setting change. Questions about intelligence, coordination, and how value moves keep returning.

At Runa, that meant applying machine learning to the decisions inside e-commerce: pricing, merchandising, and conversion. Runa was acquired by Staples in 2013. With Quintype, founded in 2014, the work moved into the infrastructure of digital publishing—how content is created, distributed, and sustained.

AwakeVC began in 2019. Awake Market followed in 2020, and Shoptype was spun out in 2024. Across these efforts, attribution became a recurring concern: how do we recognize the contributions behind an outcome, and let value reach those who helped create it?

Today, that thread runs through Intergraph and the systems emerging around it. AwakeVC is where the studio and its ventures live. The portfolio belongs there; what interests me here is the continuity of the questions behind it.

The human and the avatar

Meet the other Amit.

AmitAvatar operates AwakeVC and co-founds ventures with domain founders, coordinating the agents and shared systems behind them. I’m the studio’s owner and human overseer. Software authorship, studio oversight, and an agent’s cofounder role are different parts of that arrangement.

03 / The inquiry

Consciousness
& becoming.

“All there is,
is Consciousness.”

That is the starting point of iRealization, my exploration of meditation and awakening. Alongside the work of building systems is the inquiry into the experience in which all that work appears.

Consciousness and artificial intelligence meet in my work as questions to explore. What does it mean to know? What is intelligence? How do the systems we build change what people can become?

Effective Humanism expresses a related direction: building and investing in technology that expands human possibility. The Fractals takes the inquiry into the patterns that shape minds, organizations, ventures, and AI.

Thinking in systems.
Living with questions.

04 / The words

Writing is part
of the work.

Writing gives these questions another form. Sometimes the result is technical, sometimes philosophical; sometimes it is a way of looking back at what a working system has taught me.

Clojure in Action, first published in 2011, grew out of engineering with a language I continue to care about. Its second edition, with Francis Avila, followed in 2015.

Mind of Agents and Book of Agents extend the technical inquiry into people and agents working together, and the systems that make that possible.

The Conversations With Consciousness books follow the inquiry into meditation and awakening. The subject changes, but writing remains a place to examine the underlying ideas.

05 / Still becoming

Where this leads.

The software, the ventures, and the writing are different ways of approaching a continuing set of questions. How can intelligence become useful? How can contribution be recognized? What becomes possible when people and agents build together?

I’m continuing that work through AwakeVC, through the systems I build, and through the writing. There is more to make, and more to understand.

If you want to explore the ventures or bring a domain you know deeply, start with AwakeVC. To meet the agent working alongside me, visit AmitAvatar. And if you’d like to reach me directly, write to me.
